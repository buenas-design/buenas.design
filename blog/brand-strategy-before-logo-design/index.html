Don't design a logo before answering these 5 questions

Most people come to us asking for a logo. But before the logo comes brand strategy: five questions about who your brand is for, what makes it different and where your business is going.

By Julia Sonda · November 1, 2026 · 3 min read

Almost every first conversation at the studio starts the same way: I need a logo. And we get it. The logo is the part you can see. It goes on the sign, the bag, the Instagram profile. It feels like the brand.

But a logo designed without strategy is like packing a suitcase without knowing where you're flying. You might pack beautiful things. You just won't know if they're the right ones until you land.

So what is brand strategy, really?

It sounds like a big corporate word, so let's make it small. Brand strategy is the set of decisions that gives every visual choice a job. Before we draw anything, we want clear answers to five questions:

  • Who is this brand for? Not everyone. The specific person who lights up when they find you.
  • What do they feel before they meet you, and what should they feel after?
  • Why you? What makes you different from the other five tabs they have open?
  • What's the personality? If your brand walked into a room, how would it talk, dress and laugh?
  • Where is the business going in the next three to five years?

When those answers are clear, design stops being a matter of taste. Colors, typography, shapes, photography and words all get a direction to follow, and every choice gets a reason behind it.

Brand strategy in a real project

The story behind Poppy Snowdrop Paper Co is full of sorrow and loss. Knowing that changed the brief completely. The identity had a job, and that job was to bring joy, warmth and authenticity to something born in a hard season. So the florals became playful, the palette became generous, and the whole visual universe was built to make people smile.

Without that context, we could have made something pretty. With it, we made something true. That's the difference strategy makes: a trend tells you what's popular this year, strategy tells you what's right for you.

The quiet cost of skipping brand strategy

Skipping strategy rarely looks like a disaster on day one. It looks like small things that pile up:

  • The logo looks lovely on its own, but nothing else seems to match it.
  • Every new post, menu or flyer turns into a fresh debate about colors and fonts.
  • The brand looks a little like everyone else in your niche, because it was built from your niche's references.
  • A year later, it already feels like time to change it.

That last one is the expensive one.

Being good isn't enough. You have to look good, and look like yourself.

How we draw the map at Buenas

Strategy is the first stop of every brand design journey with us. It starts with a free discovery call, where we listen to your story, your clients and your plans. Then comes an in-depth brand questionnaire, the kind you answer calmly at home with a coffee. And before any logo exists, we present concepts and talk through the visual direction together, so the route is agreed before we start designing.

Only then do we open the sketchbook. And when we finally present the identity, we can tell you why every curve, color and word is there.

Your first stop

If you're building a brand, or you feel that yours no longer tells the truth about your business, start with the map. Take the five questions above, grab a notebook and answer them, even if the answers come out messy. You'll be surprised how much clearer the road gets.

And if you'd like company on the way, that's exactly what we're here for.
