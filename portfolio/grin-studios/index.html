Grin Studios Jewelry

Grin Studios is a modern jewelry brand that offers luxurious, sustainable and affordable moissanite jewelry. It was inspired by moissanite itself, a stunning and ethical alternative to diamonds, and its handcrafted pieces are made for women who appreciate luxury, ethics and accessibility.

For the logo we chose a typeface that is elegant and modern, with a few customized letters, and paired it with an icon that sparkles like moissanite and like the glow inside everyone who wears it. The colors make a playful palette that still feels refined, and the botanical elements, a nod to the eco-friendly origins of moissanite, add a lively, organic touch that speaks to younger and more mature women alike.
